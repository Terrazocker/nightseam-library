Panorama

Take a 360° panorama from your telescope spot (e.g. phone pano mode), upload it, calibrate, then paint over trees/houses.

Calibration

FOV < 360° for partial panoramas. Azimuths outside the image are unblocked in obstacle mode, fully blocked in sky mode.

Crop (sides only)

Drag horizontally to select full-height bands to remove from the left and/or right edge — e.g. trim a 370° pano back to 360°. Cropping resets paint mask and profile.

Mask

Obstacle mode: paint over everything that blocks the sky. Sky mode: paint the open sky band instead — by default everything ABOVE the painted band is assumed free (open) sky; tick "block above band" if something above it really blocks (e.g. a balcony). Red line: obstruction below; dashed red line: obstruction above.
